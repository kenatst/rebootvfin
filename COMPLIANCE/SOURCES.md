# Sources

Official sources used for compliance foundations. Verify current versions at submission time.

## Apple
- App Store Review Guidelines: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- App Privacy details on the App Store (label questionnaire): https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/
- Privacy manifest files (required-reason APIs): https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/privacy_manifest_files
  - Required reason API list: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/privacy_manifest_files/describing_use_of_required_reason_api
- Family Controls / Screen Time APIs:
  - FamilyControls: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/familycontrols
  - DeviceActivity: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/deviceactivity
  - ManagedSettings: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/managedsettings
  - Requesting the distribution entitlement: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/entitlements/com_apple_developer_family-controls
- StoreKit 2: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit
- Auto-renewable subscriptions review requirements: https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-subscriptions/overview-of-subscriptions
- Standard EULA default terms: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/

## EU / privacy regulation
- GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679): https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj
- UK GDPR / Data Protection Act 2018: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/
- EDPB guidelines portal: https://www.edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/general-guidance/guidelines-recommendations-best-practices_en
- CNIL (France-specific guidance): https://www.cnil.fr/en/professionals-page
- Consumer rights directive (withdrawal/refund context for digital services): https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32011L0083

> Note: drafts intentionally do NOT claim certification ("GDPR certified", "Apple approved") anywhere user-facing or in metadata.
