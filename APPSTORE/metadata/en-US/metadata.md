## Description
Your attention is pulled apart by every notification, feed, and interruption. REBOOT trains it back.

**HOW IT WORKS**
Each day REBOOT prescribes one short practice: staying with a single task, returning after a distraction, recalling what you just read. Your honest reflections become evidence. The program adapts to what actually helps *you*.

**A PROGRAM, NOT A TRICK LIST**
- 90 days across six phases: Calibrate, Control Input, Build Stability, Deepen, Find Flow Conditions, Own the System.
- Miss a day? Nothing is lost. There are no streaks and no guilt.
- Free training modes stay available anytime.

**IT LEARNS YOU**
- Sessions reveal your real focus window — not what you guess about yourself.
- Experiments test whether a habit genuinely helps you, with clear KEEP or DROP answers.
- Environment tools (optional Screen Time protection) reduce digital friction during practice.

**THE FINISH LINE**
Day 90 ends with your Attention Operating Manual: what breaks your attention, how you return, which conditions hold your deepest work — built from your own recorded evidence, not averages. After that, Own Mode keeps it useful without an endless program.

**PRIVACY BY DESIGN**
Everything stays on your device. No account, no cloud, no analytics, no tracking.

**PLEASE NOTE**
REBOOT trains attention behaviors. It is not a medical product and does not diagnose or treat any condition.
