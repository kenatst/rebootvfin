## 1. Scope

This policy describes how REBOOT — a 90-day attention-training application for iOS — handles information. REBOOT is designed so that your personal data stays on your device.

## 2. What REBOOT processes and where it is stored

REBOOT stores the following **locally on your device**, in the app's own sandbox:

- Your answers to the onboarding diagnosis questions.
- Your attention profile, derived priors, and evidence maturity states.
- Session records: mode, duration, switches, return behavior, difficulty, energy context, and free-text reflections you choose to write.
- Personal rules, experiment designs and outcomes (Personal Lab).
- Flow projects, block plans, and block evidence you create.
- Fuel context entries you provide (energy, sleep, meal and movement context).
- Digital Environment settings: Screen Time selections stored as opaque tokens, Focus Windows, intervention logs, interruption events.
- Weekly review records, guidance history, your Attention Operating Manual content, notification preferences.

None of this is transmitted to the operator or to any third party. REBOOT has no accounts, no servers receiving user data, no cloud sync, no analytics SDKs, and no advertising SDKs.

## 3. Apple Screen Time / Family Controls

If you grant Screen Time authorization, REBOOT uses Apple's Family Controls, Device Activity and Managed Settings frameworks to shield apps you select during focus sessions. The app selections are stored as opaque tokens that REBOOT cannot decode into app names or identities; they are never logged or transmitted. Device Activity monitoring runs by Apple's system, on-device. You can revoke this authorization at any time in iOS Settings.

## 4. Purchases (Apple StoreKit)

Subscriptions are sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. Payment, billing, and purchase history are handled exclusively by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. REBOOT never receives your payment details. REBOOT keeps a local cached copy of your entitlement state (subscription status) so the app works offline; this cache contains no personal data beyond subscription status and expiry date.

## 5. Notifications

If you opt in, REBOOT schedules local practice reminders on your device. These are delivered by Apple's system. They are not marketing messages and contain no tracking pixels.

## 6. What leaves your device

Only what you actively share:
- Data you export via "Export my REBOOT data" using the iOS share sheet.
- Content you share yourself from the Operating Manual export.

REBOOT itself transmits nothing.

## 7. Tracking

REBOOT does not track you across apps or websites, does not access advertising identifiers, and does not request App Tracking Transparency.

## 8. Retention and deletion

Your data persists locally until you delete it. You can erase everything at any time via Settings → DATA & PRIVACY → Erase All REBOOT Data, which removes sessions, profile, rules, experiments, Flow projects, environment settings, notifications, manual content, and cached entitlement metadata from the device, cancels scheduled notifications and scheduled protections, and returns the app to its first-launch state. Uninstalling the app also removes all local data.

## 9. Security

Data is stored in the app sandbox, protected by iOS device security. REBOOT does not log personal data to system logs.

## 10. International transfers

Because REBOOT does not transmit personal data anywhere, no international transfers occur.

## 11. Your rights (EU/UK)

Under the GDPR/UK GDPR, for the data REBOOT holds (all of it local): you have rights of access ("Export my REBOOT data" produces a complete JSON copy), rectification and erasure ("Erase All REBOOT Data"), and portability (the export). As there is no remote processing of personal data by {{LEGAL_ENTITY_REQUIRED}}, no other recipient exists. Contact support@rebootattention.com for anything else.

## 12. Children

REBOOT is not directed at children under 13. It is not a medical or educational service and does not knowingly collect data from children. Because all data stays local and there are no communications features, no child data can be collected remotely.

## 13. Changes

We will update this policy if the data model changes, with an updated version number and date.

## 14. Non-medical notice

REBOOT trains attention behaviors and organizes self-observation. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any medical condition and is not a medical device.
